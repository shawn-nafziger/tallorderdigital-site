How often are flights from Cincinnati late?

20% of flights leaving Cincinnati Northern Kentucky International Airport arrive 15 or more minutes late, 2 points below the US average of 21%. That makes Cincinnati the 25th least often late of the 60 busiest US airports. Flights before 9 a.m. are late least often (13%); flights at 5 p.m. or later, most often (29%).

Why are flights from Cincinnati late?

Of every 100 minutes of delay on late flights from Cincinnati , 37 were from a late incoming plane (US: 40), 31 were the airline's own problems (US: 33) and 26 were (US: 20), as the airlines report them.

About these numbers

This page counts every domestic flight that left Cincinnati Northern Kentucky International Airport from Sep 2023 – Aug 2026, as reported to the U.S. Department of Transportation: 131,386 scheduled and 128,776 arrived. That covers the larger airlines, which must report, and the regional airlines that fly for them. A flight is late if it arrived 15 or more minutes after schedule. Every number here rests on at least 300 flights.

These are averages over three years, not today's delays. For live delays, check your airline or the FAA's airport status page.
