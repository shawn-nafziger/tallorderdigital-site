About these numbers

Every domestic flight reported to the U.S. Department of Transportation by the airlines that must report, Sep 2023 – Aug 2026, from the 60 airports with the most departures. A flight is late if it arrived 15 or more minutes after schedule; it counts against the airport it left from. These are three-year averages, not today's delays.
