NBA trivia

Five questions a day, and every answer comes from the box scores: who scored more, which team won more, who went higher in the draft. All from NBA.com stats for the six regular seasons from 2020-21 through 2025-26.

Published Nov 24, 2026 · Seasons 2020-21 through 2025-26

How does it work?

Everyone gets the same five questions each day, starting with an easy one. Pick an answer and the numbers behind it appear, with a link to the player's or team's page on the site's NBA dashboards. When you're done, share your score as a row of squares without giving the answers away. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

Keep playing draws from all 2,793 questions in the bank, as long as your streak lasts. Totals are regular-season totals, and every answer shows how many games each player played, since a total depends on games played as much as on skill.

Sample NBA trivia questions and answers

Ten questions from the bank, none of which will turn up in a daily set. Open each one for the answer.

  1. Who scored more points in the 2024-25 regular season?Ivica Zubac · Deni Avdija

    Answer: Ivica Zubac. Ivica Zubac had 1,340 points in 80 games that season. Deni Avdija 1,219 in 72 games. See Ivica Zubac this season

  2. Who had more steals in the 2021-22 regular season?Donovan Mitchell · Derrick White

    Answer: Donovan Mitchell. Donovan Mitchell had 99 steals in 67 games that season. Derrick White 64 in 75 games. See Donovan Mitchell this season

  3. Which team won more games in the 2025-26 regular season?Golden State Warriors · New Orleans Pelicans

    Answer: Golden State Warriors. In 2025-26: Golden State Warriors 37–45 and New Orleans Pelicans 26–56. See the Golden State Warriors this season

  4. Who blocked more shots in the 2020-21 regular season?Naz Reid · John Collins

    Answer: Naz Reid. Naz Reid had 76 blocks in 70 games that season. John Collins 63 in 63 games. See Naz Reid this season

  5. Who had more assists in the 2025-26 regular season?Austin Reaves · Evan Mobley

    Answer: Austin Reaves. Austin Reaves had 280 assists in 51 games that season. Evan Mobley 234 in 65 games. See Austin Reaves this season

  6. Where did Russell Westbrook play before the NBA?Oregon · UCLA · Wake Forest · SMU

    Answer: UCLA. Russell Westbrook came to the NBA from UCLA. More NBA stories

  7. Who was picked earlier in the 2021 NBA draft?Franz Wagner · Josh Giddey

    Answer: Josh Giddey. Josh Giddey went 6th overall; Franz Wagner went 8th. See Josh Giddey this season

  8. Which of these players had the most assists in the 2020-21 regular season?Domantas Sabonis · Chris Paul · Jaylen Brown · Bam Adebayo

    Answer: Chris Paul. Chris Paul had 622 assists in 70 games that season. Domantas Sabonis 415 in 62 games, Jaylen Brown 195 in 58 games and Bam Adebayo 346 in 64 games. More NBA stories

  9. Who made more 3-pointers over the six regular seasons from 2020-21 through 2025-26?Klay Thompson · Tyrese Maxey

    Answer: Klay Thompson. From 2020-21 through 2025-26, Klay Thompson had 1,101 3-pointers and Tyrese Maxey 916. See Klay Thompson this season

  10. Which of these players scored the most points in the 2021-22 regular season?Julius Randle · Scottie Barnes · Stephen Curry · Jalen Green

    Answer: Stephen Curry. Stephen Curry had 1,630 points in 64 games that season. Julius Randle 1,447 in 72 games, Scottie Barnes 1,134 in 74 games and Jalen Green 1,157 in 67 games. See Stephen Curry this season

Where do the answers come from?

Every answer is worked out from data before it reaches the page, and a question is only asked when the gap between the choices is big enough to matter: at least 40 points, 15 assists, 20 rebounds, 8 threes, 6 blocks or 6 steals, or 2 wins.
