Weather trivia

Five questions a day, and every answer comes from the record books: daily highs, lows, rain and snow for 323 US cities going back as far as 1843, plus the sun's own math for daylight.

Published Nov 17, 2026 · Data through Nov 15, 2026

How does it work?

Everyone gets the same five questions each day, starting with an easy one. Pick an answer and the numbers behind it appear, with a link to the city's full record. When you're done, share your score as a row of squares without giving the answers away. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

Keep playing draws from all 3,822 questions in the bank, as long as your streak lasts.

Sample weather trivia questions and answers

Ten questions from the bank, none of which will turn up in a daily set. Open each one for the answer.

  1. Which city has the colder all-time record low?El Paso, TX · Norfolk, VA

    Answer: El Paso, TX. El Paso, TX's record low is −8°F on January 11, 1962, in records back to 1879. Norfolk, VA's is −3°F on January 21, 1985, in records back to 1874. See El Paso in the Weather Almanac

  2. Which city has the hotter all-time record high?Boston, MA · Honolulu, HI

    Answer: Boston, MA. Boston, MA's record high is 104°F on July 4, 1911, in records back to 1872. Honolulu, HI's is 95°F on September 19, 1994 (a reading it has hit twice), in records back to 1940. See Boston in the Weather Almanac

  3. Which city has hotter July afternoons?Billings, MT · Detroit, MI

    Answer: Billings, MT. Billings, MT averages 87.1°F on July afternoons. Detroit, MI: 83.9°F. See Billings in the Weather Almanac

  4. Which city has colder January nights?Chicago, IL · Spokane, WA

    Answer: Chicago, IL. Chicago, IL averages 17.4°F on January nights. Spokane, WA: 23.9°F. See Chicago in the Weather Almanac

  5. Which city gets more snow in a typical year?Milwaukee, WI · Portland, OR

    Answer: Milwaukee, WI. Milwaukee, WI averages 50 in of snow a year. Portland, OR: 4.4 in. See Milwaukee in the Weather Almanac

  6. Since 2000, has Hartford, CT set more or fewer daily record lows than a steady climate would?More · About as many · Fewer

    Answer: Fewer. Hartford, CT has set 50 daily record lows since 2000. A steady climate would give about 81. Record cold is getting rarer there. Read: Record lows are disappearing

  7. How much daylight does Utqiagvik (Barrow), AK get on the shortest day of the year?None · 0 h 35 min · 1 h 10 min · 1 h 35 min

    Answer: None. On December 21, Utqiagvik (Barrow), AK gets no daylight at all (the sun doesn't rise). On June 21 it gets 24 hours (the sun doesn't set). Explore a year of daylight

  8. Which city gets more daylight on the shortest day of the year?Dallas, TX · Syracuse, NY

    Answer: Dallas, TX. On December 21, Dallas, TX gets 9 hours 59 minutes of daylight and Syracuse, NY 8 hours 59 minutes. In winter, the farther south you are, the longer the day. Explore a year of daylight

  9. Which of these cities gets the most rain and melted snow in a typical year?Amarillo, TX · Austin, TX · St. Louis, MO · San Antonio, TX

    Answer: St. Louis, MO. St. Louis, MO averages 42 in of rain and melted snow a year. Amarillo, TX: 20 in, Austin, TX: 36 in and San Antonio, TX: 32 in. See St. Louis in the Weather Almanac

  10. Which city gets more rain and melted snow in a typical year?Des Moines, IA · Little Rock, AR

    Answer: Little Rock, AR. Little Rock, AR averages 50 in of rain and melted snow a year. Des Moines, IA: 37 in. See Little Rock in the Weather Almanac

Where do the answers come from?

Every answer is worked out from data before it reaches the page, and a question is only asked when the gap between the choices is big enough that a rounding difference can't flip it.
