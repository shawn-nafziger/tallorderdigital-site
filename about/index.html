About

Shawn Nafziger

I'm a business intelligence and data-visualization analyst. I build dashboards and reports in Power BI and Tableau, and Tall Order Digital is where I do my own work.

This site is for data stories: questions worth asking, answered with real data and drawn so you can see the answer. I start with the NBA because basketball is full of numbers people argue about, and most of those arguments come down to the same thing: how much can you trust what you're seeing?

Each story says where its data comes from, how much of it there is, and where the answer is uncertain. New to the numbers? Stats, explained walks through each one in plain words, with basketball examples.

Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't collect your name, email or anything else that identifies you.

Two cookie-free services count visits. GoatCounter counts which pages people read, and whether they open a glossary term or reach the end of a story. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures how fast pages load. Neither one follows you to other sites.
