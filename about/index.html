Tall Order Digital

This site is for data stories: questions worth asking, answered with real data and drawn so you can see the answer.

It starts with the NBA because basketball runs on stats and strong opinions, and the two usually meet at the same question: how much can you trust what you're seeing?

Each story says where its data comes from, how much of it there is, and where the answer is uncertain. New to the numbers? Stats, explained walks through each one in plain words, with basketball examples.

Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, and reading it doesn't collect your name, email or anything else that identifies you.

If you sign up for the weekly email, your address goes to Buttondown, the service that sends it, along with the page you signed up on. It's used only to send that email, and never sold or shared. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Two cookie-free services count visits. GoatCounter counts which pages people read, and whether they open a glossary term, reach the end of a story, use a story's share button or send the contact form below. A shared link ends in ?ref=share, so visits from shares can be counted too; it doesn't say who shared it. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures how fast pages load. Neither one follows you to other sites.

If you use the contact form below, your name, email and message go to Formspree, the service that delivers them by email. They're used only to reply to you, and never sold or shared.
