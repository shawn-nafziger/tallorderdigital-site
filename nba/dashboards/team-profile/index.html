How to read this

  • Every rank is out of 30 and "1st" is always best for this team, so the best defense is 1st even though its number is lowest.
  • Colors only mark gaps bigger than luck. Early in the season most bars stay gray, and that's the honest answer.
  • Shot making swings a lot. Hot and cold shooting, by a team or its opponents, is the noisiest part of a team's numbers and usually fades.

What it doesn't say

Who was injured, who was resting, and how good each opponent really is. Strength of schedule uses opponents' results so far, which are just as noisy as this team's. Location-based shot quality also ignores how closely each shot was defended.
