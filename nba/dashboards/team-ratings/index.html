How to read this

  • The shaded band on each row is the : how far an exactly average team could drift by chance after that many games. It's wider for teams that have played fewer games, and it shrinks every week.
  • A dot outside its band is a start that luck alone rarely produces. It still isn't proof: injuries, schedule and opponents all matter early.
  • The estimate view blends this season with last season, which is the better guide until about game 6.

Why the caution

Over the last five seasons, a team's net rating after 4 games predicted the rest of its season only loosely ( 0.42). It became a good guide, 0.7, around game 14. Defensive rating was the noisiest, barely passing 0.5 on average. The full test is in The Early-Season Mirage.
