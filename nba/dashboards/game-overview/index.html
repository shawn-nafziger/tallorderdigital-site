Factors: from each team's box score, as reported by the NBA. Edge mark: a gap of 0.15 on a scale where 1.0 is a 95th-percentile gap from 2025-26 games. Zones: only compared when both teams took at least 5 shots there. Source: NBA Stats API via nba_api.

How to read this

  • The headline names the factor with the biggest edge, measured against how big that factor's gaps usually get. Different factors swing by different amounts, so each gap is divided by a big gap for that factor.
  • An edge needs a scaled gap of 0.15 or more. Smaller gaps aren't mentioned.
  • Shots by zone only compares zones where both teams took at least 5 shots.

What it doesn't say

The Four Factors explain how a margin was built, not who played well. A team can win the shooting battle and still lose the game on turnovers and rebounds.
