How to read this

  • Ranks are among qualified players (), so a hot start in a few minutes doesn't top a list.
  • Colors only mark gaps bigger than luck, using one rule everywhere: more than twice the normal chance wobble.
  • Role numbers are choices, not luck. Minutes and usage changes come from coaches and teammates, so they're shown without luck colors.

What it doesn't say

Defense, beyond what shows up in on/off. Injuries and who else was on the floor. Shot quality by location ignores how closely each shot was defended, and plus-minus mixes in teammates and opponents.
