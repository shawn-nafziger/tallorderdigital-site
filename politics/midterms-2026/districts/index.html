The House, district by district

Where did each party win House seats: big cities or small towns, richer or poorer places? Here are all 435 districts, with the Associated Press's calls so far.

  • Big metro areas: of the 206 districts where most people live in a big metro area, AP has called 144 for the Democrat and 24 for the Republican.
  • Small towns and rural areas: of the 112 districts where most people live in small towns or rural areas, AP has called 74 for the Republican and 18 for the Democrat.

How to read this page

Every chart here is about places: House districts, the people who live in them, and how their races have been called.

A rural district doesn't tell you how each rural person in it voted. Profiles describe everyone who lives there, including people who didn't vote.

How this was put together

  • Districts: all 435 on the lines used in 2026, including the nine states that drew new maps. For those states, district profiles were rebuilt from neighborhood (block group) data; their median age and income are close estimates.
  • Where people live: from the 2020 Census. Each district's people are split by the size of the urban area they live in: big metro (1 million people or more), mid-size city (50,000 to 1 million), small town (5,000 to 49,999) or rural (outside any urban area).
  • No race calls: this page reports AP's calls with their sources and says who leads in unofficial counts. Counts are unofficial until each state's .
  • Places vs. people: district profiles describe everyone who lives there, including people too young to vote and people who didn't vote. They don't say how any group of people voted.
