# Tall Order Digital website: notes for Claude

Public site for Shawn Nafziger's data stories. The project memory, data and analysis live in private repos: `shawn-nafziger/nba-analytics` (NBA stories) and `shawn-nafziger/weather-intelligence` (weather stories). Read the relevant repo's `CLAUDE.md` for who Shawn is and how we work.

## Rules
- **SEO standards (Nov 10, 2026): read `docs/seo-standards.md` before adding or changing any page, template, address or publishing step**, and follow its pre-publish checklist. It applies here and in every repo that builds pages for this site. Plan, decisions and checklist of open work: `docs/seo-plan.md`.
- **Changes arrive as pull requests.** Shawn reviews and merges; the **Publish site** workflow (`.github/workflows/publish.yml`, Nov 10, 2026; Pages source "GitHub Actions") publishes `main` plus the Almanac on every push, in about a minute. If it fails, nothing is published and the live site stays as it was; check the repo's Actions tab.
  - **One exception, the daily dashboard publish (decided Oct 30, 2026):** every morning Shawn commits the dashboard pages, `nba/dashboards/team-ratings/index.html`, `nba/dashboards/game-overview/index.html`, `nba/dashboards/team-profile/index.html`, `nba/dashboards/player-profile/index.html` (added Nov 2, 2026) `nba/dashboards/hot-cold/index.html` (added Nov 3, 2026) and `nba/weird-stat/index.html` (the weird stat of the day, added Nov 8, 2026), straight to `main` with no PR. They are generated in `nba-analytics`, whose build scripts check the data before writing. The exception covers only those `index.html` files and only that daily data refresh. Everything else still comes as a PR, including the share images (`nba/dashboards/*/share.png`, `nba/weird-stat/share.png`) and the dashboards index (`nba/dashboards/index.html`). Changes to a dashboard's layout or design are made in the `nba-analytics` template. Don't hand-edit the dashboard pages here: the next morning's publish overwrites them.
- **Static only:** self-contained HTML, data baked in. No server, database, login or build step. The publish workflow only copies files (this repo plus the Almanac's built pages); keep it that way.
- **Relative links only** (`about/`, `../../`), never `/about/`, so pages work both at the GitHub Pages project address and on the custom domain. Exception: `404.html` (served from any depth).
- **No NBA or team logos.** Names, abbreviations and stats are fine.
- **Credit the data source on every page.** NBA stories keep the "not affiliated with the NBA" line in the footer; weather stories credit NOAA instead.
- **Copyright line in every footer (Nov 9, 2026):** `<p>© 2026 Tall Order Digital LLC. Charts and text may be shared with credit and a link.</p>`, on its own line; any footer links go on the next line. Story templates and dashboard templates in the data repos and the Almanac templates in `weather-live/almanac/` carry the same line; change them together (decision in `docs/brand-decisions.md`).
- **Every published claim follows `knowledge/reference/methods.md`** in the story's data repo: honest sample sizes, ranges where uncertain (and, for NBA, totals not averages).
- **Story addresses:** `/<topic>/<slug>/`: weather stories at `weather/<slug>/`, NBA stories at `nba/<slug>/` (e.g. `nba/role-call-minutes-usage/`). The slug is the story's name plus one or two plain keywords, lowercase with hyphens. **Never break a published address:** when a page moves, leave a redirect page in the old folder (meta refresh plus `location.replace` to the new relative path, `noindex`, a canonical link and the story's link-preview tags pointing at the new address), and update the home card. Example: `stories/phoenix-after-dark/`.
- **Story pages are generated** from `<repo>/analysis/<story>/` in the data repos. Edit the template there and re-run, then copy the output here; don't hand-edit the copy.
- **Site structure (Nov 8, 2026): topic pages, not one mixed list.** NBA publishes far more often than anything else, so each topic has its own page and nothing is listed twice: **`nba/`** (NBA stories, the Latest Tall Take, NBA dashboards), **`weather/`** (the Almanac link and weather stories) and **`stories/`** ("More stories": politics, travel, culture, nature and everything else; the `culture/` folder and its filter button were added Nov 12, 2026, first story `culture/blockbusters-got-longer/`, generated in `misc-analytics/analysis/film_blockbusters/`; the `nature/` folder and its filter button the same day, first story `nature/houseplants-native-homes/`, generated in `misc-analytics/analysis/houseplants/`). The home page shows three doors (NBA, Weather, More stories) with each page's newest pieces; the doors are the same size, and the NBA door shows the game summaries, the weird stat and the newest focus story (`extra` and `homeSeries` in `tools/build-hubs.js`; Nov 8, 2026); its old `?topic=` links forward to the matching page. Decision record: `docs/brand-decisions.md` → Site structure.
- **Story cards live on their topic page**, between `<!-- cards:start -->` / `<!-- cards:end -->`: NBA cards on `nba/index.html` (links relative to `nba/`, e.g. `href="role-call-minutes-usage/"`), weather on `weather/index.html`, everything else on `stories/index.html` (links like `../politics/...`). Every card has a topic and a date in its kicker; NBA and weather cards also carry a **series**: `<a class="story" data-topic="nba" data-series="check-the-take" ...>`. Series so far: NBA `check-the-take`, `focus`; weather `month-in-review`, `record-watch`, `focus` (listed in `SERIES` in `tools/build-hubs.js`). Add a card anywhere in its list, then run **`node tools/build-hubs.js`** in the same PR (`--check` reports anything out of date): it sorts each list newest first, rewrites each NBA and weather card's kicker as "<series> · <date>" ("Focus story · Oct 29, 2026"), and writes the generated parts (the "Browse by series" cards, which link to each series and list no stories, the `nba/check-the-take/` list, the NBA page's Latest Tall Take and Dashboards boxes, which sit side by side under the intro, and the home doors). Each Latest list shows the newest 5 with a "Show all N stories" button (`data-limit` on the filter group); choosing a series shows all of it. Then `node tools/search-index.js`, `node tools/structured-data.js` and `node tools/sitemap.js`. `tools/sort-home.js` is gone.
- **Filters above each list** (`assets/topic-filter.js`): by series on `nba/` and `weather/` (`?series=check-the-take`), by topic on `stories/` (`?topic=politics`). A button with no stories hides itself; a new series or topic needs one more button.
- **Series pages:** `nba/check-the-take/` (every Check the Take, newest first; written by `build-hubs.js`). A new series page gets its own folder under the topic and a `page` entry in `SERIES`.
- **Search (`search/`, added Nov 2, 2026)** runs in the browser from an index baked into `search/index.html`: story and dashboard titles and summaries, each Stats, explained section, and the About page. **Rebuild it with `node tools/search-index.js` in the same PR whenever a story, dashboard or Stats, explained section is added or retitled** (`--check` reports whether it's current). Daily dashboard refreshes don't need it.
- **Dashboards hub (`dashboards/`, added Nov 2, 2026; out of the menu since Nov 8, 2026, linked from the home page, and each topic page shows its own dashboards):** one card per subject: NBA dashboards (`nba/dashboards/`, which links back with "← All dashboards") and the Weather Almanac at `https://tallorderdigital.com/weather/almanac/` (kept absolute, as the hub's one off-repo card: the search index reads it that way, and the folder isn't in a local copy). **The Almanac (Nov 10, 2026)** is built daily in `weather-live`, which pushes the pages to its `almanac-build` branch and asks this repo to republish; **`.github/workflows/publish.yml`** copies that branch into `weather/almanac/` at publish time, so the Almanac is an ordinary folder of the site (same hosting and certificate). The old `weather.tallorderdigital.com` addresses 301 there through Cloudflare redirect rules on that subdomain only. Never add a `weather/almanac/` folder to this repo: the publish stops if one exists. It is open to search engines, with its own sitemap at `weather/almanac/sitemap.xml`, listed in `robots.txt`. A new subject gets a new card (`<a class="hub-card" data-topic="...">`); off-site cards are picked up by the search index automatically.

Menu (Nov 8, 2026): NBA, Weather, More stories ("More" on narrow phones), then a thin divider (Nov 9, 2026: topics on the left, site links on the right; `.nav-about`), About, Subscribe (outlined; envelope icon on phones), then the search icon and the light/dark button. The tool writes the whole menu each time, works out the path to the site root from the logo link (`class="brand"`) and marks the current section (for story templates in the data repos, by the repo they're in). The **Almanac** templates in `weather-live` (`almanac/template.html`, `almanac/index_template.html`; Nov 9, 2026) carry it too, with the main site's address as `{{MAIN}}` and Weather marked current; they keep their own light/dark script (it also redraws their charts), which the tool detects and leaves alone. Story templates in **politics-analytics and travel-analytics** carry the menu too: run the tool on them when those repos are next worked on (never the subscribe parts on the travel template). Master copies: `assets/site-header.css` and `assets/site-header.js`. Site pages link them; self-contained pages (stories, dashboards and their templates in the data repos) carry inline copies written by `python3 tools/apply-header.py` (no arguments = every page here except the daily pages, the dashboards and the weird stat; or pass template paths). - **Analytics on every page** except redirect pages: the block before `</body>` (GoatCounter for visits and reader events, Cloudflare Web Analytics for page speed; both cookie-free) plus the footer privacy line linking to `about/#privacy`. Story templates in the data repos carry the same block, so new stories copied from a template get it. Dashboards: tallorderdigital.goatcounter.com and Cloudflare → Web Analytics.

## Design
Logo kit in `assets/brand/` (see its README; updated Oct 29, 2026): four bars, tallest first. The tall bar is orange #F99A2B; the three shorter bars step down and fade toward the background (light: #0A3746, #366F80, #62A8BB; dark: #7FC4D6, #4F8D9E, #2E6272). Wordmark in Barlow Semi Condensed: "TALL ORDER" Ink #201914 / Paper #F3F4EF on dark, "DIGITAL" Bark #4A403A / Stone #DDDAD3. The header and home hero draw the mark as inline SVG (bar classes `m-o`, `m-2`, `m-3`, `m-4`; colors from the `--logo-*` tokens) so it follows light/dark mode; story templates in the data repos carry the same rules. The logo plays an animated intro once per visit, on the first page a reader lands on: the hero on the home page, the header logo anywhere else (Nov 9, 2026; skipped with reduce motion). It is part of the header: code in `assets/site-header.js`, hide rule in `assets/site-header.css`, and a guard line in `<head>` after the saved-theme line, all written by `tools/apply-header.py`, so templates run through the tool carry it. Details in `docs/brand-decisions.md` → Animated logo. Logo shades are not chart colors. No other logos.

**Read `docs/design-system-and-dataviz-playbook.md` before designing anything.** This repo's `docs/` folder is the **master copy** of the playbook, `docs/brand-decisions.md`, `docs/seo-standards.md` and `docs/seo-plan.md`. Copies in Claude Projects are reference only; if one disagrees with `docs/`, `docs/` wins. Update `docs/` in the same pull request as any design change, so the docs always match what's live.

**Who owns what:**
- **Sitewide (owned by the "Tall Order Digital" Claude Project):** `assets/site.css` tokens, the light/dark theme and toggle (`assets/theme.js`), header, footer, navigation, logo kit and favicon, the home, About and 404 pages, and `docs/`.
- **Stories (owned by the weather and NBA data repos and their projects):** story pages, their templates and charts. Stories use the shared tokens, header and logo rules as they are; they don't define new colors or change the shell. If a story needs a new sitewide token or component, add it to `docs/` and `assets/site.css` first, in its own PR.

**Palette (rolled out Oct 29, 2026):** ink on paper.
- **Light:** Paper `#F3F4EF` background, white cards, Ink `#201914` text, Bark `#4A403A` secondary.
- **Dark:** Deep `#03222C` background, Teal `#0A3746` cards, Paper text, Stone `#DDDAD3` secondary.

**Chart roles** (tokens in `assets/site.css` and each story template; story data colors come only from these):
- `--amber` = signal: Burnt `#B36000` light, Orange `#F99A2B` dark
- `--edge` = at the edge: Peach `#E3B88A` light (a pale tint of Burnt, so it reads weaker than the signal; changed from Orange on Oct 29), `#8F6230` dark
- `--ghost` = noise: `#CDC9C1` / `#3A5762`
- `--navy` = comparison: Teal / Sky `#7FC4D6`
- `--funnel` = bands: Sky at 25-30%
- `--ink-3` = small labels: derived `#6E645D` / `#9FB1B8`, 4.5:1 or better

**Color rules:**
- **Text wears text colors.** A highlighted number stays ink, with a small colored chip beside it (classes `.big`, `.near`, `.up`). Light orange is never used for text.
- **Color keys:** orange = the real signal, light orange = "at the edge", gray = noise. Each key goes on its own line, starting with the color word.

**Type:** Barlow Semi Condensed (headlines, big numbers, logo), IBM Plex Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (data and labels).

**Light/dark toggle:** the first visit follows the reader's system setting; the sun/moon button (last in the menu) switches and saves the choice in localStorage (`tod-theme`), and every page reads that choice before drawing. `tools/apply-header.py` puts all three pieces on a page (the saved-choice line after `<meta charset>`, the `.theme-toggle` button, and its styles and script), so it goes wherever the header goes: site pages link `assets/theme.js`; self-contained pages get inline copies between `theme-toggle:start` / `theme-toggle:end` markers. Master copies: `assets/theme.js` and the `.theme-toggle` rules in `assets/site.css`. Every page has it as of Nov 4, 2026. **Story templates in the data repos must be run through the tool too** (`python3 tools/apply-header.py path/to/template.html`), or a rebuilt story drops the button; `--check` on a template reports whether it's current.

**Share cards** (`tools/share-cards.js`) use the dark palette: Deep background, Sky comparison, Orange signal, and the logo's on-dark bar colors.
