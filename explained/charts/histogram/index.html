What it is

Values sorted into bins, with one bar per bin showing how many items fall in it. It shows the shape of the spread.

How to read it

Tall bars are common values. Look at the overall shape: one hump, two, or a long tail to one side.

Use it whenThe shape of one measure across many items.
Skip it whenA few dozen items (use a beeswarm or dot plot), or comparing many groups (use box plots).
Watch outBin width changes the picture. And when a few giants dominate, an ordinary scale puts nearly everything in one bar: try a log scale.
