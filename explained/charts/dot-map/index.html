What is a dot map?

One dot per place, at its real location, sized by how big it is.

How to read a dot map

Look for clusters and gaps first, then the big dots. Size is area, so a dot twice as wide is four times as big.

When to use a dot map

Use it whenThings that are points: plants, stores, airports.
Skip it whenRates for areas like states (use a choropleth); or thousands of overlapping dots.
Watch outBig dots hide small ones, and thousands of dots merge into one blob: switch to Whole U.S. above to see it. Zoom in (as the state view does), draw the small dots on top, and offer a table.
