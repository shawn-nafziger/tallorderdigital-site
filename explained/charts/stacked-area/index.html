What is a stacked area chart?

Lines stacked on top of each other and filled, so the top edge is the total and each band is one part of it.

How to read a stacked area chart

The top edge is the total. Only the bottom band sits on a flat baseline, so read it precisely; judge the others roughly, by thickness.

When to use a stacked area chart

Use it whenShowing the total and its rough mix over time at once.
Skip it whenWhen readers need exact values for the upper bands (use a line chart), or when parts go negative.
Watch outA band can look like it's shrinking just because the band under it wiggles. Put the most important part at the bottom.
