What it is

A map where each area is shaded by a value. Here, each state by its share of wind and solar.

How to read it

Darker states have more. Look for regional patterns, but remember that size isn't value.

Use it whenRates or shares for areas: percent, per person, per square mile.
Skip it whenCounts: big states would look important just for being big. And points, like power plants.
Watch outBig, empty states grab the eye. Montana looks more important than New Jersey here. The tile grid map fixes that.
